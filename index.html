FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

What is a carbon footprint?

A carbon footprint represents the greenhouse gas emissions associated with activities, products, or lifestyles.

How can I reduce my carbon footprint?

You can start with practical changes such as reducing unnecessary travel, saving energy, reducing waste, and making more sustainable food choices.

Is recycling really effective?

Recycling can help recover materials and reduce the need for some new resources when materials are properly collected and processed.

What are renewable energy sources?

Renewable energy comes from naturally replenishing sources such as sunlight, wind, water and geothermal heat.

How can I live more sustainably on a budget?

Many sustainable actions can also save money, such as reducing food waste, using less energy, walking, cycling, or using public transportation.
